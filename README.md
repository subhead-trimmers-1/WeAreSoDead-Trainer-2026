## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **WE ARE SO DEAD**. This trainer enhances the supernatural co-op gameplay of WE ARE SO DEAD by giving players greater control over the sunrise countdown, ghost abilities, relic searches, demon encounters, and floor progression. It is designed for private sessions and supports the game's central communication and survival mechanics.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore all nine floors, learn demon behavior, practice ghost communication, collect relics safely, or replay the co-op horror experience with reduced time and survival pressure.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Freeze Sunrise Countdown** — Stops the sunrise timer so the group has more time to explore, communicate, collect relics, and prepare the banishment ritual.
- **Ghost Safety Mode** — Protects living players from fatal encounters while preserving the ghost and seance gameplay loop.
- **Reveal Relic Locations** — Highlights the ritual relics that ghosts must locate and guide living players toward.
- **Enable Ghost Abilities** — Improves access to ghost actions such as flickering lights, opening or closing doors, passing through living players, and using loud screams.
- **Unlock Floor Progression** — Allows access to deeper floors of the haunted house without completing every previous banishment ritual.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Reveal Demon Information** — Displays useful information about the current demon, including its hidden behavior and danger patterns.
- **Reveal Demon Name** — Shows the demon's true name once the required relics have been collected, making the final banishment sequence easier to complete.
- **No Death Penalty** — Prevents player deaths during dangerous encounters while retaining the game's ghost communication and exploration systems.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Living players move through the haunted house while avoiding dangers that may be visible only to ghosts. Ghosts can move freely, pass through living players, and observe threats from a safer perspective. Use ghost guidance to steer the group toward safe routes and objectives.

### Tools & Inventory

The living team must transport the ritual relics back to the seance board. Ghosts can locate the relics but cannot physically carry or manipulate them, so communication is essential. Use the relic-location option to simplify searches and coordinate each return trip.

### World & Environment

The game takes place across nine floors beneath a haunted house. Each floor contains a different demon, procedural elements, hidden dangers, and a banishment objective. Completing the ritual unlocks the next floor, with deeper levels increasing the challenge and changing the available threats.

### Combat & Weapons

WE ARE SO DEAD has no conventional weapon-based combat. Survival depends on recognizing demon behavior, avoiding hazards, gathering the correct relics, and spelling the demon's true name at the seance board. Players who die become ghosts and can continue helping the living through supernatural observation and communication.
